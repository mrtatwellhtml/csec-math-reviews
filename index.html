    Good habits

    • Use a sharp pencil; thick lines lose marks.
    • Keep every arc and construction line. Do not rub them out.
    • Measure lengths to the nearest mm and angles to the nearest degree.
    • “Same radius” means do not touch the compass screw or legs.
